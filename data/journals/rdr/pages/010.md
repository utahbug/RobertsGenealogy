## English transcription

Logan City January 27th 1865.

On the eight (8th) of this month, January,
mother Thomas and myself started for Salt Lake City
to receive our Endowment in the house of the Lord we
arrived in the City on the tenth (10th) on the thirteenth
(13th) we went throught the house and was ordained to
an office of an Elder Thomas by Peter Maughan myself
by [crossed out: Peter Sprague; interlined: James L Sprague] a High priest mother throught the house
she was sealed to our father by Wilford Woodruff.

On the morrow we started for home arrived on
the sixteenth (16th) in good health though the weather was
very cold we found all well at home.

Logan City Jan. 23rd 1867.

December 9th 1866 Ann our sister
started to Salt Lake City to receive her Endowment
in the house of the Lord she was married and sealed
to a young man by the name of William Hopkin December

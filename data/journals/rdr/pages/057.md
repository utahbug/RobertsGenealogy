## Editorial annotations

First-pass visual transcription completed. The family narrative contains difficult syntax, crowded dates, marginal writing, and several uncertain place/name readings; these are preserved without reconciliation.
The manuscript clearly gives John Roberts's death date as `November 15th 1856` in two places. External evidence identifies him as John Robert Roberts (born December 8, 1822), Robert D. Roberts's uncle, who traveled with his family in the Edward Bunker handcart company and arrived in Salt Lake City on October 2, 1856. The Church History Biographical Database reports October 16, 1856 (RID `PER-D4ML88N`) but supplies no death-specific citation. Boyd D. Roberts's later family history, *The Family of John Robert Roberts and Mary Richards*, states that Salt Lake County death records give October 16 and that cemetery information places the burial in Plat F, Block 10, Lot 7; the underlying record image was not independently verified in this pass. A different John Roberts, a 42-year-old pipe maker from Bristol in the Willie handcart company, is independently recorded in that company's contemporary journal as dying on October 16 while still on the trail. The two men are distinct, and no direct evidence of profile conflation was found, but the provenance of October 16 for the Bunker-company John remains unresolved. The manuscript date is preserved unchanged.

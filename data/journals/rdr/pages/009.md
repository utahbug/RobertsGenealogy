## English transcription

work for the Army on a farm and make some Adobies
for them I earn little money and return back to mother,
brothers and sisters we payed most all of our debt my
brother thus had earn some wheat.

In the latter part of October I started North
to look for some land that was not occupied that we
mite be able to cultivated and raise our own wheat and
potatos I arrived in Cache Valley this Valley was newly and
thinly settled I stayed in Logan and got some land
here I hired and work out for a little while my brother
Thomas came after me he made an agreement with a
man by the name of Shephard to drive a team for
him back to Farmington and again back to Cache
Valley he work a month for this man I went back
home about the same time when I was returning back
to Cache Valley Shephard hired me to drive a herd of
sheep for him Thomas and myself was traveling
together this time we had the most terrible
weather that any of us ever witness in our lives it
was freezing & blowing that I never so the like but we
cross the mountain we put up a house this winter
in the month of February the year 1860 we return
home the family was all well we had to stay here
because our team was to weak we was short of hay
to feed them in the latter part of March we commence
our journey to Cache Valley it was two weeks before we
arrived in Logan Daniel stayed in Farmington to work
and in a short time came after us we did not raise
but a small crop wheat 59 bus. Barley 4 bus. Patch
15 bus. Potatos in this with few other things we have
prosper very well since the year 1856 until the
present it was not very easy for us to raise much
of a crop because we had to fence the land and make
ditches to carry the water at present we have a
log house with three rooms and we live happily.

January 9th 1861.

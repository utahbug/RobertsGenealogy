Mai 22, 1876

Hanah Roberts fy ngwraig, am bum munud wedi deuddeg canol dydd (5 wedi 12), a roddodd enedigaeth i fachgian bach yn pwyso bedwar pwys (4 lbs). Mae yn edrych yn eitha iach, ond y mae yn lled wan. Wrth edrych arno mi a dybiaf, yn cael ei feithrin, y daw yn fachgian doeth a dysgedig. Bendithiwyd ef gan Georg Painter yr un dydd; ei enw Hugh Roberts.

## Modern English translation

[continuation from PDF page 88]

and a part in the first resurrection, by Joseph F. Smith.

Robert Roberts to Elizabeth Edwards.

Griffith Morris to Elizabeth Roberts.

Proxies: Robert David Roberts and Ann Roberts Hopkin.

Thomas Richards to Ann Morris.

Richard Thomas to Elizabeth Thomas.

Thomas Williams to Cathreine Ych Richards.

Rowland Morris to Jane Parry.

John Thomas to Elinor Morris.

Robert Richards to Elizabeth Morris.

Henry Morris to Cathreine Morris.

Proxies: Robert David Roberts and Cathreine Thomas Roberts.

May 22, 1876

At five minutes past twelve noon, Hanah Roberts, my wife, gave birth to a little boy weighing four pounds. He looks quite healthy, but he is rather weak. Looking at him, I think that, if he is nurtured, he will become a wise and educated boy. He was blessed by Georg Painter that same day; his name is Hugh Roberts.

June 1, 1876

Sarah Cathreina Hopkin was baptized by Charles Olson; she was confirmed under Christian L. Larson.

July 10, 1876

At half past seven in the morning, Sarah Ann, the wife of Daniel Roberts in Ogden, [continues on PDF page 90]

## Editorial annotations

- The opening completes the sealing list begun on page 88. It has a confirmed close parallel on RDR PDF page 16. The Welsh order, name forms, and proxy grouping are preserved independently.
- The May and June 1876 entries have confirmed close-paraphrase parallels on RDR PDF page 17. RDR normalizes `Hanah` to `Hannah` and `Georg` to `George`; the Welsh source forms remain unchanged here.
- The July 1876 entry continues onto page 90 and has a confirmed close parallel on RDR PDF page 17.
- Handwriting continuity and first-person family language support probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears on this page.

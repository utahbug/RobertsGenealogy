## Editorial annotations

Page 69 continues the Daniel Roberts and Gwen Lloyd family entry from page 68, proceeds through several Roberts-family notices, and includes a four-line verse associated with a death in Elinor's family. It continues toward page 70, where the same compact Welsh family narrative and hand remain visible.

The page has extensive ink loss, abrasion, erasure, and overwriting. The upper genealogy and lower Vermont passage are substantially legible, but a broad middle band and portions of the verse are not recoverable from surviving letterforms. Damage markers represent manuscript loss; words from RDR have not been supplied as Welsh text.

RDR PDF pages 3-4 provide a confirmed historical-English parallel and help establish the order of the Daniel, John, Edward, Robert, and Elinor family notices. RDR contains fuller prose and names than the damaged Welsh page, so it is linked as a close translation/paraphrase with expansion and normalization, not used to reconstruct missing manuscript wording. The Welsh list visible here appears to differ from RDR's fuller list of John and Mary Roberts's children; the source-specific forms are preserved.

The verse occupies four visually distinct lines and is in the same compact hand and ink as the surrounding entry. Its introductory clause is damaged, and no surviving wording establishes a connection to the hymn attributed to `D. R.` in the 1852 Welsh LDS hymnal. It is therefore treated as an unidentified damaged verse, possibly commemorative, rather than identified as a known hymn.

The first-person wording and continuity from the dated `Fy Hanes` entry on page 68 support David Roberts as content author. Despite local damage, the surviving hand matches the directly signed `David R Roberts` entries dated February 4 and April 6, 1858, confirming David Roberts as scribe within the limits of historical manuscript-image comparison. No secondary hand or later insertion is apparent.

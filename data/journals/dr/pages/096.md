# journal-dr-page-096

## Welsh transcription

Mawrth 22ain 1881

Am chwech yn yr hwyr Jane Hughes ein chwaer a rodd enedigaeth i ferch fechan yn pwyso 4 pwys. Mae y fam a'r ferch yn cymeryd yn dda. Ganwyd hi yn Denbigh, Ontario County, Belle Farmley.

Logan City Hydref 21ain 1881

Sarah Ann Roberts, gwraig Daniel Roberts, am bymtheg munud wedi tri yn y bore (3.15 a.m.) a rodd enedigaeth i ferch lan yn pwyso deg pwys a haner (10 1/2 lbs). Bendithiwyd hi Rhagfyr 5ed gan Christian L. Larson, ei henw Ann Roberts.

Logan City Rhagfyr 26ain 1881

Margaret Goodman, gwraig i Thomas J. Roberts, am bum munud a phumdeg wedi saith yn y bore, a rodd enedigaeth i fab glan yn pwyso naw pwys. Bendithiwyd ef gan Thomas McNeil Mawrth 2ail 1882, ei enw David William Roberts.

Logan City Tachwedd 13eg 1882

Yn y Trydydd Ward ordeiniwyd David Robert Roberts gan Robert Davidson i swydd o Ddiacon yn yr Offeiriadaeth Aaron.

## Modern English translation

March 22, 1881

At six in the evening, our sister Jane Hughes gave birth to a little girl weighing four pounds. The mother and daughter are doing well. She was born in Denbigh, Ontario County, Belle Farmley.

Logan City, October 21, 1881

At fifteen minutes past three in the morning (3:15 a.m.), Sarah Ann Roberts, wife of Daniel Roberts, gave birth to a fine daughter weighing ten and a half pounds. She was blessed on December 5 by Christian L. Larson and named Ann Roberts.

Logan City, December 26, 1881

At fifty-five minutes past seven in the morning, Margaret Goodman, wife of Thomas J. Roberts, gave birth to a fine son weighing nine pounds. He was blessed by Thomas McNeil on March 2, 1882, and named David William Roberts.

Logan City, November 13, 1882

In the Third Ward, David Robert Roberts was ordained by Robert Davidson to the office of deacon in the Aaronic Priesthood.

## Editorial annotations

- This page begins a new series of dated family and church entries and does not continue the Margaret Goodman Davies biography on page 95.
- RDR PDF page 22 is a confirmed close-paraphrase parallel. RDR adds Margaret Hughes's later blessing and name after the March 1881 birth, information not present in this Welsh entry.
- The Welsh October heading appears to read `Hydref 21ain 1881`; RDR gives October 22, 1881. The sources have not been harmonized.
- The Welsh writes `Robert Davidson`; RDR writes `Robert Davieson`.
- Handwriting continuity supports probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears.
- No occurrence of `bach` or a distinct Robert-derived nickname is visible.

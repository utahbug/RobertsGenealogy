# journal-dr-page-093

## Welsh transcription

[continuation from PDF page 92]

Rhagfyr 29, 1878

Jane ein chwaer, gwraig Conar Hughes, yn byw yn Samaria, a rhodd enedigaeth i fachgan yn edrych yn dda ac yn gryf am bum munud i wyth yn y bore. Ei enw yw Conar Hughes.

Ebrill 3, 1879

Bedyddiwyd David Robert Roberts gan William Waterson; cadarnhawyd ef gan Robert Davidson yr un dydd. Ac hefyd bedyddiwyd David Roberts Hopkin gan Christian L. Larson, a chadarnhawyd ef gan Henry Ballard yr un dydd.

Mai 26, 1879

Am ddeg munud wedi saith yn y bore (10 wedi 7), Ann Hopkin ein chwaer a rodd enedigaeth i fachgen cryf a bywiog yn pwyso deg pwys (10 lbs). Bendithiwyd ef gan Christian L. Larson Gorffenaf 3, 1879; ei enw William John Hopkin.

Mehefin 23, 1879

Am ychydig o funudau i un o'r gloch y prydnawn bu i David Taliesin Hughes farw o glefyd y gwddf ar ol ychydig o dri diwrnod. Aeth yn sal yn yr ysgol fore Gwener a bu farw.

Hyrum Hughes a clafychodd o'r un clefyd ar yr unfed ar hugain (21) o'r un mis [continues on PDF page 94]

## Modern English translation

[continuation from PDF page 92]

December 29, 1878

Jane, our sister and the wife of Conar Hughes, living in Samaria, gave birth to a boy who looked well and strong at five minutes to eight in the morning. His name is Conar Hughes.

April 3, 1879

David Robert Roberts was baptized by William Waterson and confirmed by Robert Davidson that same day. David Roberts Hopkin was also baptized by Christian L. Larson and confirmed by Henry Ballard that same day.

May 26, 1879

At ten minutes past seven in the morning, Ann Hopkin, our sister, gave birth to a strong and lively boy weighing ten pounds. He was blessed by Christian L. Larson on July 3, 1879; his name is William John Hopkin.

June 23, 1879

A few minutes before one in the afternoon, David Taliesin Hughes died of a throat disease after an illness of about three days. He became ill at school on Friday morning and died.

Hyrum Hughes became ill with the same disease on the twenty-first of the same month [continues on PDF page 94]

## Editorial annotations

- The opening closes the February 1878 entry begun on page 92, then four distinct dated entries follow.
- The December 1878, April 1879, and May 1879 entries have confirmed close-paraphrase parallels on RDR PDF page 20.
- The manuscript heading for the Hughes death entry appears as `Mehefin 23, 1879`; RDR gives June 28. Both are preserved as a textual disagreement rather than harmonized.
- The Hughes death entry continues onto page 94 and has a confirmed close parallel on RDR PDF page 20.
- Handwriting continuity and first-person family language support probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears.

## Modern English translation

Logan City, January 27, 1885

The names below were baptized by [unclear in Welsh: Lle Thomas]. They were confirmed by Charles C. [unclear: Pen] in the Temple. Proxy: Robert D. Roberts.

- William Hughes — married to my mother's female cousin
- Griffith Jones
- William Jones — my mother's male cousin
- Robert Williams — my mother's male cousin
- Richard Thomas — my mother's brother
- Morris Isaac — my mother's brother-in-law
- Robert Roberts
- Edward Roberts — my father's brother
- Henry Jones — my father's male cousin
- William Jones — [unclear relationship notation]
- John Williams — my father's male cousin
- David Jones
- Thomas Jones
- Henry Jones — son of Thomas Jones
- Owen Williams — my male cousin
- Edward Griffith — my father's grandfather
- Robert Parry — my father's brother-in-law
- William Humphry
- John Humphry
- Ebenezer Humphry — [unclear group relationship]

April 16, 1889

I was baptized for William Humphrey, the husband of my father's sister. Baptizer: Arvid Hurst; confirmed by Niels C. Edlyson.

## Editorial annotations

- Page 106 has two structures: a January 1885 shared proxy-baptism list with individual kinship annotations, followed by a separate April 1889 baptism record.
- Genealogy and ordinance data remain separate: the heading supplies baptism, confirmation, temple, and proxy context; kinship terms belong only to their written name lines.
- Explicit terms include `yn briod a` (married to), `cyfnither` (female cousin), `cefnder` (male cousin), `brawd` (brother), `brawd ynghyfraith` (brother-in-law), `mab` (son), and `daid` (grandfather).
- RDR PDF page 31 is a confirmed close-paraphrase/normalized parallel for the January list. Its opening officiator wording and the William/Henry Jones relationships are also uncertain, so RDR was not used to force those Welsh readings.
- The April 1889 record has no clear parallel on RDR page 31 and is retained as a separate manuscript witness.
- Handwriting continuity supports probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears.
- No occurrence of `bach` or a distinct Robert-derived nickname is visible.

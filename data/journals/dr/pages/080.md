## Modern English translation

[Continuation from PDF page 79]

...well from the year 1859 until now. It was not easy for us to raise much of a crop because we had to fence and enclose the land. We now have a house with three rooms, and we live happily.

January 9, 1861.

R. D. Roberts

Logan City, January [unclear: 29], 1865

On the eighth of this month (that is, January), my mother, Thomas, and I set out for Salt Lake City to be washed, anointed, and blessed in the house of the Lord.

We reached the city on the tenth. On the thirteenth we went through the House and were ordained Elders: Thomas by Peter Maughan and I by [crossed out: Peter Sprague; interlined: James L. Sprague]. My mother and I went through the House, and she was sealed to my father by Wilford Woodruff.

The following day we turned homeward and reached home on the sixteenth in good health. Although the weather was very cold, we found everything well at home.

R. D. Roberts

## Editorial annotations

First direct visual transcription and modern translation completed. The page contains three distinct entries.

The opening lines complete Welsh PDF page 79 and correspond to RDR PDF page 9 with confirmed confidence. The relationship is a close translation with paraphrase. Welsh gives 1859; RDR gives 1856. The immediate context makes the Welsh 1859 reading unequivocal as written, but does not establish which historical chronology is correct.

The January 1865 temple entry corresponds to RDR PDF page 10 with confirmed confidence. It is a close translation with paraphrase and minor expansion in RDR. The Welsh heading appears to read January 29, while RDR gives January 27; the source readings remain separate. Both witnesses preserve the Peter Sprague deletion and James L. Sprague interline.

The March/April 1865 marriage-family entry has no clear RDR parallel. It continues onto Welsh PDF page 81 and is not harmonized with later RDR family chronology. Its relationship wording is transcribed conservatively and remains subject to review.

The hand, slant, spacing, line density, and signature forms are continuous across the page. Two explicit R. D. Roberts signatures confirm him as scribe and content author for the first two entries. The same hand probably records the final entry, but its content-author attribution remains uncertain because no parallel witness has yet been identified.

## Modern English translation

[continuation from PDF page 81]

Taliesin Hughes's mother died in the State of Illinois, America, and his father is now there, having married another woman. These children, namely Taliesin, his brother, and his sisters, were brought out by their uncle William Jones, their mother's brother. After coming to Utah Territory, to Willard in Box Elder County, he departed after some time and went back to Illinois, and brought out his sister's children, seven in number. Their father promised to come after them, but he has not yet come. Taliesin Hughes and Elizabeth, our sister, live in a house of his own, and everything appears well.

R. D. Roberts

On April 14, in the year 1868, a daughter was born to Ann, our sister, and her husband William Hopkin; her name was Sarah Cathrina Hopkin.

I received in a letter from my uncle Edward Roberts, my father's brother, dated July [unclear: 20], 1854, the day, month, and year when David R. Roberts, Cathrina his wife, and their children were born. It had been in the possession of David R. Roberts, where he (David), with his own hand, had entered the first record of his children.

David R. Roberts was born April 5, 1814.

Cathrina, [crossed out: June] July 25, 1828.

Their children:

Robert, September 21, 1839

Thomas, July 6, 1840

Daniel, September 8, 1842

Anne, December 19, 1844

Elizabeth, November 15, 1846

Jane, January 11, 1850

Robert D. Roberts

## Editorial annotations

- The opening completes the February 4, 1867 entry begun on PDF page 81 and ends with an explicit `R. D. Roberts` signature. It has a confirmed parallel across RDR PDF pages 10-11. RDR closely paraphrases and expands the migration account.
- The Welsh appears to state that Taliesin Hughes's mother died in Illinois. RDR page 10 instead says that his mother lived in Illinois. Both readings are preserved as a material textual disagreement; RDR has not been used to override the Welsh.
- The April 14, 1868 birth notice has a confirmed close parallel on RDR PDF page 11. The Welsh source form `Sarah Cathrina Hopkin` is preserved.
- The family register has a confirmed close parallel on RDR PDF page 11. The page identifies Edward Roberts's July 1854 letter as the immediate source and says that David R. Roberts had entered the children's record in his own hand. Robert D. Roberts is the present page's scribe; David R. Roberts is the content author of the underlying Bible register.
- The letter's July day is not fully secure from the Welsh letterforms and remains `[unclear: 20fed]`; RDR supplies July 20 but is not treated as decisive. The overwritten month for Cathrina is represented as written rather than silently normalized.
- The handwriting remains continuous with the signed Robert D. Roberts hand on page 81. Explicit signatures after the migration account and at the bottom of the page support confirmed scribal attribution.

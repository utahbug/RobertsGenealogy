## Welsh transcription

rhod enedigaeth i eneth yn edrych a chyfansoddiad cryf, yn pwyso naw pwys (9 pwys). Hydref 5 bendithiwyd hi yn Logan gan Thomas Jones; ei henw yw Cathreina Roberts.

Rhagfyr 29, 1876

Jane Hughes ein chwaer a rhodd enedigaeth i fachgan am bymtheg munud wedi naw (9.15) yn y bore yn Samaria, Sir Oneida, Tiriogaeth Idaho. Bendithiwyd ef Chwefror 11, 1877, gan Owen Thomas; ei enw James Hughes. Pwysiwyd ef yn fis oed, 11 lbs.

Ionawr 23, 1877

Am bum munud ar hugain wedi un ar ddeg (11.25) yn y bore, Elizabeth Hughes ein chwaer, gwraig Taliesin Hughes, a rhodd enedigaeth i eneth yn Samaria, Sir Oneida, Tiriogaeth Idaho. Bendithiwyd hi Chwefror 18 gan Owen Thomas; ei henw Maria Hughes.

Mai 16, 1877

Daniel yn Ogden y bore hediw a dderbyniodd y newydd truanus o farwolaeth ein hanwyl chwaer Elizabeth gyda'r telegram o Ddyffryn Mallad, Samaria. Ef yn arwain ar y cerbyd a ddygodd Brigham Young a'i gyfeillion i Logan rhwng deuddeg ac un o'r gloch; fe a ddaeth a'r newydd calarus i'r teulu yn y rhan hon o'r wlad. Bu ein hanwyl chwaer i ymadael a'r fuchedd hon am chwech o'r gloch yn y bore yn esmwyth a thawel. Mi roedd yn dymuno ar i'w theulu i ddod i rhoi ei gweddillion i orwedd yn y ddaear hyd fore yr adgyfodiad. Ar y 17eg cychwynasom ein taith tuag yno; cawsom dymhestl [continues on PDF page 91]

## Modern English translation

[continuation from PDF page 89]

gave birth to a girl who appeared to have a strong constitution, weighing nine pounds. On October 5 she was blessed in Logan by Thomas Jones; her name is Cathreina Roberts.

December 29, 1876

Jane Hughes, our sister, gave birth to a boy at fifteen minutes past nine (9:15) in the morning in Samaria, Oneida County, Idaho Territory. He was blessed on February 11, 1877, by Owen Thomas; his name is James Hughes. At one month old he weighed eleven pounds.

January 23, 1877

At twenty-five minutes past eleven (11:25) in the morning, Elizabeth Hughes, our sister and the wife of Taliesin Hughes, gave birth to a girl in Samaria, Oneida County, Idaho Territory. She was blessed on February 18 by Owen Thomas; her name is Maria Hughes.

May 16, 1877

Daniel, in Ogden this morning, received by telegram from Malad Valley, Samaria, the sorrowful news of the death of our beloved sister Elizabeth. He was conducting the carriage that brought Brigham Young and his companions to Logan; between twelve and one o'clock he brought the grievous news to the family in this part of the country. Our beloved sister departed this life at six o'clock in the morning, peacefully and quietly. She desired her family to come and lay her remains in the earth until the morning of the resurrection. On the seventeenth we began our journey there; we encountered a storm [continues on PDF page 91]

## Editorial annotations

- The opening completes Sarah Ann Roberts's July 1876 birth entry from page 89. The December 1876 and January 1877 entries are separate family notices. All have confirmed close-paraphrase parallels on RDR PDF page 17.
- The May 1877 Elizabeth Hughes death narrative is a separate entry and continues onto page 91. It has a confirmed close historical parallel on RDR PDF page 18.
- RDR expands punctuation and some travel context. The Welsh manuscript remains the authority for its wording and chronology.
- Handwriting and first-person narrative continuity support probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No secondary hand or signature appears.

## Welsh transcription

[continuation from PDF page 93]

ac a fu farw am ddau o'r gloch y bore 25. Mi roedd ef clu yn glefyd. Wedi ei eni Mawrth 6, 1874, mae'r ddau yn fechgyn braf iawn ac yn ymddangos y buasent yn tyfu i fechgyn cryf. Claddwyd hwy yn Samaria, Oneida, Tiriogaeth Idaho, lle y mae eu tad yn byw.

Logan City, Hydref 4, 1879

Sarah Ann Roberts, gwraig Daniel Roberts, a rhodd enedigaeth i ferch fechan yn yr hwyr am un o'r gloch y prydnawn, yn pwyso 9 lbs. Rhagfyr 4 bendithiwyd hi gan Thomas McNeil; ei henw yw Ida Roberts.

Awst 5, 1880

Sarah Ann, gwraig Daniel Roberts, a fedyddiwyd ar unwaith gan Christian L. Larson. Cadarnhawyd hi gan Henry Ballard.

Chwefror 19, 1881

Thomas J. Roberts a ail fedyddiwyd gan Henry Ballard; cadarnhawyd ef gan yr un. Ac ar y 24ain, fe a'i amcanwraig Margaret Davies a aethant i Dy yr Arglwydd yn Ninas y Llyn Halen, a unwyd yn nghwlwm y cyfamod tragwyddol i fod yn wr a gwraig am amser ac am dragwyddoldeb gan Daniel H. Wells.

Margaret yn cael ei hindowment ar yr un pryd [continues on PDF page 95]

## Modern English translation

[continuation from PDF page 93]

and died at about two o'clock in the morning on the twenty-fifth. He had been very ill with the disease. Born on March 6, 1874, the two were fine boys who appeared likely to grow into strong young men. They were buried in Samaria, Oneida County, Idaho Territory, where their father lives.

Logan City, October 4, 1879

Sarah Ann Roberts, the wife of Daniel Roberts, gave birth to a little girl at about one o'clock in the afternoon, weighing nine pounds. On December 4 she was blessed by Thomas McNeil; her name is Ida Roberts.

August 5, 1880

Sarah Ann, the wife of Daniel Roberts, was rebaptized by Christian L. Larson. She was confirmed by Henry Ballard.

February 19, 1881

Thomas J. Roberts was rebaptized by Henry Ballard and confirmed by the same man. On the twenty-fourth, he and his intended wife, Margaret Davies, went to the House of the Lord in Salt Lake City and were united in the bond of the everlasting covenant as husband and wife for time and all eternity by Daniel H. Wells.

Margaret received her endowment at the same time [continues on PDF page 95]

## Modern English translation

Logan City, May 16, 1877

Brigham Young and his companions came here, and on the seventeenth the place was measured for laying the foundation of the Temple. On the eighteenth the eastern [ground] was first broken and the place was dedicated, and on September 19, 1879, [unclear in Welsh: foundation stones were placed] at every corner, with John Taylor presiding. On May 17, 1884, it was completed and dedicated. A meeting was held for three days, and I had the privilege of going inside on the second [unclear in Welsh]. After it had been dedicated, each day was for visitors, who had the opportunity to go through most of the rooms.

Logan City, January 21, 1885

Work for the dead began in the Logan Temple. I had been baptized for thirty-three in Salt Lake City on October 13, 1875. I received the endowment for my father.

I was confirmed by Thomas Moore for:

January 21 — David Robert Roberts — by Fredrig Pine

22 — Robert Roberts — [ditto] by Milton D. Hammond

23 — Thomas Richards — [ditto]

28 — Edward Griffith — by Thomas Cordon

## Editorial annotations

- The page has two distinct chronological and structural segments. The May 1877 heading begins a retrospective Logan Temple construction/dedication account that extends through May 1884; the January 1885 heading begins a dated proxy-confirmation list.
- The chronological jump is therefore a copied or retrospective historical record, not evidence that the page was physically written in 1877. The manuscript does not state its immediate source.
- RDR PDF page 24 is a confirmed paraphrase/expansion parallel for both segments. RDR clarifies the broad temple chronology and ordinance context but does not resolve every Welsh word.
- The proxy list preserves the manuscript's name and date order. Relationship and ordinance details have not been inferred from adjacency.
- Handwriting continuity supports probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears.
- No occurrence of `bach` or a distinct Robert-derived nickname is visible.

Logan City, Gorphenaf 12, 1870

Mehefin 1, 1870, yn cychwyn i Smithfield i nol Hannah Roberts, tac yn dod yn ol i Logan. Tranoeth yn cychwyn i Ddinas y Llyn Halen ac yn cyrraedd yno ar y bedwaredd, 4. Yn bresennol y maent yn byw yn Smithfield, Sir Cache, Territory Utah. Ymfudasant o Eglwys bach, Sir Benfro, Cymru, i Ddinas y Llyn Halen yn y flwyddyn 1864.

Robert David Roberts

Hannah Roberts a anwyd Mawrth 27, 1847, yn Eglwys bach, Sir Benfro.

R. D. Roberts

## Modern English translation

Logan City, July 12, 1870

On June 1, 1870, I set out for Smithfield to fetch Hannah Roberts and returned to Logan. The next day I set out for Salt Lake City and arrived there on the fourth. On the sixth we went to the House of the Lord to be united for time and eternity by Joseph F. Smith, one of the Apostles and a son of Hyrum Smith the patriarch. Hugh and Mary Roberts are Hannah's parents. At present they live in Smithfield, Cache County, Utah Territory. They emigrated from Eglwys bach, Pembrokeshire, Wales, to Salt Lake City in the year 1864.

Robert David Roberts

Hannah Roberts was born March 27, 1847, at Eglwys bach, Pembrokeshire.

R. D. Roberts

January 4, 1871

Ann, our sister, gave birth to a fine son at six [unclear in Welsh: in the morning], weighing ten pounds. His name was David Roberts Hopkin. He was blessed by [unclear: Benjamin M. Munnon] on May 4, 1871.

March 22, 1871

Elizabeth, our sister, gave birth to twin girls on February 8, 1871, at Farmington, Utah, Davis County. Their names were Ann and Margaret. Ann was born at forty minutes past nine in the evening (9:40); Margaret at thirty minutes past ten (10:30). Ann weighed five and three-quarter pounds (5 3/4 lbs); Margaret five and one-quarter pounds (5 1/4 lbs). [Sentence continues on PDF page 85]

## Editorial annotations

- Page 84 contains three dated entries. The July 1870 marriage and family-history entry and the January 1871 Hopkin birth notice are complete; the February 1871 twins account continues onto page 85.
- The July entry has a confirmed close-paraphrase parallel on RDR PDF page 12. RDR supplies additional travel phrasing but preserves the same chronology, relationships, Eglwys bach origin, and 1864 migration year.
- The January entry has a confirmed parallel on RDR PDF page 12. RDR reads the blessing name as `Benjamin M. Lewis`; the Welsh letterforms on this page remain uncertain and are not forced to match RDR.
- The twins entry has a confirmed close parallel on RDR PDF page 12. The Welsh page independently writes the Farmington, Utah, Davis County location and preserves the names Ann and Margaret.
- The handwriting remains continuous with the signed Robert D. Roberts group on page 83. The first-person marriage narrative, `Robert David Roberts` self-identification, and `R. D. Roberts` signature support confirmed content-author and scribal attribution.

# journal-dr-page-072

## Welsh transcription

Elizabeth a fedyddiwyd Ebrill 9 - 1855.

Y mae Robert heb ei fedyddio er ei fod yn credu yr Efengyl. Ac y mae Jane heb ei fedyddio oherwydd ei bod o dan oedran.

Ganwyd fy Ngwraig yn y Creua Mawr, Llanfrothen. Enw ei Thad, fel y soniwyd uchod, Thomas Richards; enw ei mam, Ann Morris. Ei Thad a fagwyd gyda ei nain yn y Creua. Enw ei dad oedd Richard Thomas; enw ei fam oedd Elizabeth. Enwau ei daid a'i nain oedd Thomas Williams a Chathreina uch Richard.

Enwau plant Richard ac Elizabeth Thomas fel y canlyn: Thomas, Chathreina, Robert a Jane Richards.

Enwau plant Thomas Richards ac Ann Morris fel y canlyn: Jane, Chathreina a Richard Thomas.

Ann Morris, gwraig Thomas Richards, a anwyd yn Llwyn yr Hwch, Llanfrothen. Enw ei Thad Rowland Morris; enw ei mam oedd Jane Parry. Enwau plant Rowland a Jane Parry fel y canlyn: Rowland, Janet, Ann, Elinor, Henry Morris.

Enwau fy Nhad a mam ydynt Robert Roberts ac Elizabeth Edwards. Hwy a briodwyd yn Beddgelert yn y flwyddyn 1806, y 3 dydd o Chwefror. Ganwyd fy Nhad mewn Cae Glas, Clynog, yn Sir Gaernarfon yn y flwyddyn 1775, ar y 18 dydd o Ionawr. Ganwyd i'w rieni naw o blant fel y canlyn: Salmon, Margred, Robert (fy Nhad), Catherine, Jane, Ann, Elinor, Elizabeth a William Roberts. Fy nhaid a anwyd yn Felin Faesog, Clynog.

## Modern English translation

Elizabeth was baptized April 9, 1855.

Robert has not been baptized, although he believes the Gospel. Jane has not been baptized because she is under age.

My wife was born at Creua Mawr, Llanfrothen. Her father, as mentioned above, was Thomas Richards; her mother was Ann Morris. Her father was raised with his grandmother at Creua. His father was Richard Thomas and his mother was Elizabeth. His grandfather and grandmother were Thomas Williams and Chathreina uch Richard.

The names of the children of Richard and Elizabeth Thomas were as follows: Thomas, Chathreina, Robert, and Jane Richards.

The names of the children of Thomas Richards and Ann Morris were as follows: Jane, Chathreina, and Richard Thomas.

Ann Morris, wife of Thomas Richards, was born at Llwyn yr Hwch, Llanfrothen. Her father was Rowland Morris and her mother was Jane Parry. The names of the children of Rowland and Jane Parry were as follows: Rowland, Janet, Ann, Elinor, and Henry Morris.

The names of my father and mother are Robert Roberts and Elizabeth Edwards. They were married at Beddgelert on February 3, 1806. My father was born at Cae Glas, Clynog, in Caernarfonshire on January 18, 1775. Nine children were born to his parents, as follows: Salmon, Margred, Robert (my father), Catherine, Jane, Ann, Elinor, Elizabeth, and William Roberts. My grandfather was born at Felin Faesog, Clynog.

## Editorial annotations

Page 72 begins by completing the children's baptism sequence from page 71: Elizabeth's baptism is dated April 9, 1855; Robert is described as believing the Gospel but not baptized; and Jane is described as unbaptized because she is under age. No officiator or confirmation is written in this continuation.

The remainder is a compact first-person family register. Its explicit groups are: Chathreina's birth, parents, and paternal grandparents; the children of Richard and Elizabeth Thomas; the children of Thomas Richards and Ann Morris; Ann Morris's parents and siblings; and David's parents, their marriage, his father's birth and siblings, and his grandfather's birthplace. Relationships are translated only where the Welsh grammar states them; adjacency has not been used to create additional kinship.

The register appears to be David Roberts's retrospective family compilation rather than a visibly copied document. It remains in the same compact Welsh hand and ink used on pages 68-71, with first-person forms `fy Ngwraig`, `fy Nhad`, and `fy nhaid`. These features support David Roberts as probable content author and probable scribe, but no source citation, signature, or independently attributable handwriting sample makes either role confirmed.

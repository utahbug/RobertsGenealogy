## Editorial annotations

- This page begins a women’s proxy-endowment list and does not continue the March 1886 men’s confirmation group on page 101. The journal has reordered record groups chronologically.
- RDR PDF page 27 is a confirmed close-paraphrase parallel, but several legible Welsh name forms differ from RDR. The Welsh appears to give `Jane Parry`, `Elinor Humphries`, and `Jane Richards`, where RDR gives Ann Parry, Elias Roberts, and Lewis Roberts respectively. The sources have not been harmonized.
- The faint sequence following Ann Roberts Hopkins remains materially uncertain. RDR's own rendering is also marked uncertain and has not been used to force the Welsh.
- Ditto marks preserve repeated proxy/ordinance context; no relationships have been inferred from list adjacency.
- Handwriting continuity supports probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears.
- No occurrence of `bach` or a distinct Robert-derived nickname is visible.

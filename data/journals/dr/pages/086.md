# journal-dr-page-086

## Welsh transcription

[continuation from PDF page 85]

unfed ar ddeg euthant i Dy yr Arglwydd, lle y cawsant eu golchi a'u heneinio, a phriodwyd hwy gan Daniel H. Wells am amser a thragywyddoldeb. Tranoeth yn cychwyn i gartref (sef Logan) ar y 15, ac ar y 21 cychwynasant i Samaria (Oneida County, Idaho Territory, Malad Valley), lle y maent yn bwriadu cartrefu.

R. D. Roberts

Mawrth 24, 1873

Mawrth 18, 1873, Jane gwraig Conar Hughes a esgorodd ar fachgen tua deg o'r gloch y boreu. Naw pwys ei bwys. Ei enw yw David Hughes. Bendithiwyd ef gan Owen Thomas, [unclear: mi a dybiaf], Mai 4, 1873.

Mawrth 6, 1874

Elizabeth ein chwaer a esgorodd ar ddau fachgen, gefeilliaid. David Taliesin Hughes a anwyd am 8.50 y boreu, a Thomas Hughes am 9.5. Bendithiwyd hwy gan David Jones a Taliesin Hughes, eu tad, Mawrth 6. Daeth mam a'r llinellau o Samaria, Mawrth 24, 1874.

Chwefror 8, 1875

Ionawr 12 daeth Jane ein chwaer a'i gwr Conar Hughes a'i bachgen bach, David, i dy fy mam, ac ar yr ail ar hugain (22) hi a esgorodd ar ei hail fab am saith munud a deugain wedi deg (10.47) y boreu. Yr oedd yn fachgen iach a dymunol, saith pwys a hanner ei bwys. Ei enw yw Daniel Hughes.

## Modern English translation

[continuation from PDF page 85]

On the eleventh they went to the House of the Lord, where they were washed and anointed, and they were married by Daniel H. Wells for time and eternity. The next day they set out for home (that is, Logan) on the fifteenth, and on the twenty-first they set out for Samaria (Oneida County, Idaho Territory, Malad Valley), where they intend to make their home.

R. D. Roberts

March 24, 1873

On March 18, 1873, Jane, the wife of Conar Hughes, gave birth to a boy at about ten o'clock in the morning. He weighed nine pounds. His name is David Hughes. He was blessed by Owen Thomas, [unclear in Welsh], on May 4, 1873.

March 6, 1874

Elizabeth, our sister, gave birth to two boys, twins. David Taliesin Hughes was born at 8:50 in the morning, and Thomas Hughes at 9:05. They were blessed by David Jones and Taliesin Hughes, their father, on March 6. Mother brought the lines from Samaria on March 24, 1874.

February 8, 1875

On January 12, Jane, our sister, and her husband Conar Hughes and their little boy David came to my mother's house, and on the twenty-second she gave birth to her second son at forty-seven minutes past ten (10:47) in the morning. He was a healthy and pleasant boy, weighing seven and a half pounds. His name is Daniel Hughes.

## Editorial annotations

- The opening completes the March 1872 marriage journey begun on page 85. It has a confirmed close-paraphrase parallel on RDR PDF page 13. RDR expands the prose but agrees on the temple marriage by Daniel H. Wells, the return to Logan, and the move to Samaria.
- The March 1873 birth entry has a confirmed close parallel on RDR PDF page 13. The short phrase before the blessing date remains uncertain in Welsh and has not been supplied from RDR.
- The March 1874 twins entry and January 1875 birth entry have confirmed close-paraphrase parallels on RDR PDF page 14.
- The page is in the same Robert D. Roberts hand and contains an explicit R. D. Roberts signature. First-person family language supports confirmed attribution to him as content author and scribe. No secondary hand is evident.

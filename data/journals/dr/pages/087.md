## Welsh transcription

Ebrill 9, 1875

Chwefror 21. Daniel Roberts fy mrawd a gafodd ei ail fedydd gan Robert Davidson; cadarnhawyd ef gan Thomas McNeil, hen.

Mawrth 27. Daniel a gychwynodd i Ddinas y Llyn Halen i briodi, a chyfarfu a'i anwylyd yn Hamilton Station. Taliesin, ei brawd, a ddaeth gyda hi o Samaria, Oneida County, Idaho. Ar y 29 euthant i Dy yr Arglwydd, lle y cawsant eu golchi a'u heneinio, a chyfamodwyd hwy am amser a thragywyddoldeb gan Wilford Woodruff. Ordeiniwyd Daniel yn Elder gan Samuel J. Smith. Cymerasant dy ar rent yn Ogden, ac ar Ebrill 3 symudasant yno.

Gorffenaf 4, 1875

Heddyw cefais fy medyddio i'r United Order gan Robert Davidson; cadarnhawyd fi gan Thomas McNeil.

Ar y 10fed Hannah Roberts fy ngwraig a gafodd ei bedyddio gan Joseph White; cadarnhawyd hi gan Robert Davidson.

Hydref 4, 1875

Heddyw cafodd mam ac Ann Hopkin fy chwaer eu bedyddio i'r United Order gan Joseph White. Cadarnhawyd Ann gan Henry Ballard, a mam gan Charles C. Card.

Hydref 16, 1875

Ar yr 8fed Ann Hopkin fy chwaer a gychwynodd i Ddinas y Llyn Halen. Ar y 9fed cychwynodd mam a minnau, a chyrhaeddasom yno ar y 10fed. Buom mewn cyfarfodydd Conference. Ar yr 11eg cefais fy mhapur dinasyddiaeth. Ar y 13eg cawsom ein bedyddio yn Nhy yr Arglwydd dros nifer o berthnasau. Ni wyddom pwy a'n cadarnhaodd. Enwau'r perthnasau [continues on PDF page 88]

## Modern English translation

April 9, 1875

February 21. Daniel Roberts, my brother, was rebaptized by Robert Davidson; he was confirmed by Thomas McNeil, senior.

March 27. Daniel set out for Salt Lake City to marry and met his beloved at Hamilton Station. Taliesin, her brother, came with her from Samaria, Oneida County, Idaho. On the twenty-ninth they went to the House of the Lord, where they were washed and anointed and covenanted for time and eternity by Wilford Woodruff. Daniel was ordained an Elder by Samuel J. Smith. They rented a house in Ogden, and on April 3 they moved there.

July 4, 1875

Today I was baptized into the United Order by Robert Davidson; I was confirmed by Thomas McNeil.

On the tenth, Hannah Roberts, my wife, was baptized by Joseph White; she was confirmed by Robert Davidson.

October 4, 1875

Today mother and Ann Hopkin, my sister, were baptized into the United Order by Joseph White. Ann was confirmed by Henry Ballard, and mother by Charles C. Card.

October 16, 1875

On the eighth, Ann Hopkin, my sister, set out for Salt Lake City. On the ninth mother and I set out, and we arrived there on the tenth. We attended conference meetings. On the eleventh I received my citizenship paper. On the thirteenth we were baptized in the House of the Lord for a number of relatives. We do not know who confirmed us.

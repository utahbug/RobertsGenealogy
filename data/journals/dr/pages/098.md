## Welsh transcription

Ionawr 19ain 1884

Robert D. Roberts a fedyddiwyd yn Farmington, Sw. Davies, Utah, Ebrill 4ydd 1858 gan fy nhad David R. Roberts. Cadarnhawyd fi gan yr un ar lan y dwr.

Ionawr 6ed 1884

Ordeiniwyd fi [unclear: ddeugainfed] er mwyn swydd (gwrwm) o'r deg a thrigain gan Silvester Low.

R. D. Roberts

Logan City Ionawr 6ed 1884

Ordeiniwyd Thomas J. Roberts i'r [unclear: ddeugainfed] cwrdd (gwrwm) o'r deg a thrigain gan Aaron Thatcher.

Rhagfyr 19eg 1883

Sarah Ann, gwraig Daniel Roberts, a rodd enedigaeth i ferch am dri chwarter wedi wyth (8.45) yn yr hwyr yn pwyso 12 lbs. Bendithiwyd hi gan Christian L. Larson yn Logan Mawrth 16eg 1884.

Logan City Mehefin 3ydd 1884

Hugh Roberts a fedyddiwyd gan William C. Partington, cadarnhawyd ef gan John Johnson yr un dydd.

## Modern English translation

January 19, 1884

Robert D. Roberts was baptized in Farmington, Davis County, Utah, on April 4, 1858, by my father, David R. Roberts. I was confirmed by the same person at the water's edge.

January 6, 1884

I was ordained [unclear technical quorum wording in Welsh] to an office in the Seventy by Silvester Low.

R. D. Roberts

Logan City, January 6, 1884

Thomas J. Roberts was ordained to the [unclear technical quorum wording in Welsh] quorum of the Seventy by Aaron Thatcher.

December 19, 1883

At a quarter to nine in the evening (8:45), Sarah Ann, wife of Daniel Roberts, gave birth to a daughter weighing twelve pounds. She was blessed by Christian L. Larson in Logan on March 16, 1884.

Logan City, June 3, 1884

Hugh Roberts was baptized by William C. Partington and confirmed by John Johnson on the same day.

## Editorial annotations

- The page contains five separate entries and does not continue the Robert Davies memorial on page 97.
- RDR PDFs 23–24 are confirmed close-paraphrase parallels. RDR identifies the January 6 ordinations with the Fourth Quorum of Seventy, but the specialized Welsh quorum wording remains uncertain and has not been forced from the English witness.
- The explicit `R. D. Roberts` signature confirms Robert D. Roberts as scribe and content author for the signed first-person entry. Strong handwriting continuity supports probable attribution for the other entries.
- The June 1884 entry ends on this page. PDF page 99 begins a distinct May 1877 historical narrative and then changes to January 1885 temple records.
- No occurrence of `bach` or a distinct Robert-derived nickname is visible.

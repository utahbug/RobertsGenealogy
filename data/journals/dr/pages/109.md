Logan City Ebrill 9ed 1888

Derbyniais lythyr o Samaria oddiwrth Conar Hughes yn ein hysbysu i fod Jane ein chwaer, ei wraig, wedi rhoi genedigaeth i ferch fach yn pwyso deg pwys Mawrth 31ain am bymtheg munud wedi deuddeg yn y bore (12.15 midnight).

Logan City Hydref 17eg 1897

Thomas D. Roberts yn cychwyn ar ei daith i Gymru, o'i [unclear: cenhadaeth hyd yn cychwyn o'r Ddinas y Llyn Halen ar y Sulfaol].

## Modern English translation

Logan City, January 5, 1895

William John Hopkins was baptized by Charles Limberg and confirmed by John Talley.

Logan City, April 9, 1888

I received a letter from Samaria from Conar Hughes informing us that Jane, our sister and his wife, had given birth to a little girl weighing ten pounds on March 31 at fifteen minutes past twelve in the morning (12:15 midnight).

We note a communication from Samaria dated May 3, 1888, informing us that the little girl born in April 1888 to Conar and our sister Jane Hughes had been blessed by Bishop Jonah Evans on May 3, 1888; her name was Cathrine Roberts Hughes.

Logan City, October 17, 1897

Thomas D. Roberts set out on his journey to Wales, from his [unclear Welsh travel/mission wording concerning departure from Salt Lake City].

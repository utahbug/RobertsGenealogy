## Editorial annotations

- This page continues the October 1875 proxy-baptism entry from page 87 and continues onto page 89 with a sealing list.
- The family lists have confirmed close historical parallels across RDR PDF pages 15–16. RDR clarifies the overall register structure but is not treated as authority for uncertain Welsh names or relationship words.
- Several opening and paternal-list words remain uncertain because of dense handwriting, overwriting, and ink loss. The page therefore remains `needs-review` despite the strong RDR parallel.
- Source forms including `Cathreine` and `Ych Richard` are preserved. RDR's English relationship labels are not silently substituted where the Welsh remains uncertain.
- Handwriting continuity and the first-person proxy statement support probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as both content author and scribe. No secondary hand is evident.

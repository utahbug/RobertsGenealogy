## Modern English translation

[Continuation from PDF page 77]

[The opening Welsh words are unclear.] My father spent the whole time watching with a man because of his feet, since his boots were too small. Thomas worked with [unclear name: James Lathad] and received ten bushels of wheat and some clothing for his work. Daniel stayed with Lot Smith and received [unclear in Welsh] for his work. Ann and Elizabeth were in service, and Jane was at home.

In 1858 we moved south with the Saints. We borrowed food from William Richard, which was repaid to him in 1859. We were in the South for nearly two months, then returned and earned a little wheat by working. My father and I went to the canyons to make shingles. After about a month my mother and Thomas were seized by an illness. My father had to remain home to watch over them, and I went to the soldiers' camp to seek work, but I did not prosper and returned. My mother and brother were no better. They began to improve after a week, but my father, Daniel, Ann, and Jane then took their place; the illness did not affect Elizabeth.

This disease ended my father's life on this earth. We buried him in the Farmington cemetery. My father died in 1858. After returning from his funeral I took to my bed and was unable to rise without help for nearly three months. In [unclear in Welsh], in the month of April...

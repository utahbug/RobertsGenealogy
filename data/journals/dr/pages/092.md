## Welsh transcription

Ebrill 10, 1878

Ymadawodd fy anwyl wraig a'r fuchedd hon yn dawel fel i gysgu. Cafodd afiechyd a phoen anghyffredin er dechreu Mawrth, efo'i dannedd i ddechreu; chwyddodd ei chorff yn debyg i'r dropsi. Roedd rhai o'r meddygon yn ei galw yn lled salach ac iawn. Ni chafodd fawr o iechyd ar ol briodi; ymsgariodd a'i bron yn agos i colli ei bywyd pob dro. Mi roedd yn edrych ac yn deimlo yn well y gaeaf hwn nag y bu ers llawer dydd; er hyn i gyd ymaflodd y dinistrwr yn ei chyfansoddiad a gwahanodd yr ysbryd a'r corff. Mi roedd yn wraig ardderchog o lan yn fan a chefnil ac yn medru ei gwaith yn eitha iawn; yn fam rhagorol iawn i'w phlant bach, yn eu dysgu i weddio ac i gydnabod Duw mor fuan ac y byddent yn bosibl; mi roedd yn gymydog cariadus a heddychlon. Ar y deuddegfed (12), am unarddeg o'r gloch (11) y bore, aethom a'i gweddillion i'r hen Hall, lle y cawsom gyfarfod da: gweddi gan Thomas McNeil; pregethau gan John Parry, Robert Davidson a Henry Ballard; diweddiad gan David W. Davies; arweiniwyd y canu gan Alizander Lewis. Claddwyd hi yn mynwent Logan; gweddiwyd gan Benjamin Williams yn y fynwent. Hwn wedi ei ysgrifenu ar y 5ed Mai.

Chwefror 21, 1878

Sarah Ann, gwraig Daniel ein brawd, a rodd enedigaeth i eneth fach am haner awr wedi chwech yn yr hwyr. Mehefin 6 bendithiwyd hi gan Thomas McNeil; ei henw yw Margaret Roberts. Mae yn edrych yn faban clws ac y cyfyd ferch lan [continues on PDF page 93]

## Modern English translation

April 10, 1878

My beloved wife departed this life peacefully, as though falling asleep. She had suffered unusual illness and pain since the beginning of March, beginning with her teeth; her body swelled as in dropsy. [Some medical wording remains uncertain.] She had little health after marrying and twice miscarried, coming close to losing her life each time. She had looked and felt better this winter than for many days, yet despite everything the destroyer took hold of her constitution and separated spirit from body. She was an excellent, clean, and thrifty wife who managed her work very well; an excellent mother to her little children, teaching them to pray and acknowledge God as soon as they were able; and a loving, peaceable neighbor. On the twelfth, at eleven in the morning, we took her remains to the old Hall, where we had a good meeting: prayer by Thomas McNeil; sermons by John Parry, Robert Davidson, and Henry Ballard; benediction by David W. Davies; singing led by Alizander Lewis. She was buried in the Logan cemetery, where Benjamin Williams offered prayer. This was written on May 5.

February 21, 1878

Sarah Ann, the wife of our brother Daniel, gave birth to a little girl at half past six in the evening. On June 6 she was blessed by Thomas McNeil; her name is Margaret Roberts. She appears to be a pretty baby who will grow into a beautiful girl [continues on PDF page 93]

- Welsh source forms `Robert Davidson` and `Alizander Lewis` are preserved despite RDR's normalized `Robert Davieson` and `Alexander Lewis`.
- The February 1878 birth entry is nonchronological and continues onto page 93. It has a confirmed close parallel on RDR PDF page 19.
- First-person spousal language and handwriting continuity support confirmed attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe, although no signature appears on this page.

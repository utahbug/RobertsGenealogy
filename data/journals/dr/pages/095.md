## Welsh transcription

[continuation from PDF page 94]

Miss Davies a aned yn Rhosllanerchrugog, Cymru, yn Sir Ddinbych, 30 Mehefin 1854.

Bedyddiwyd hi gan Henry Hatfield yn Liverpool. Cadarnhawyd gan Joseph H. Smith, Awst 1877.

Ymfudodd i Utah yn y flwyddyn 1878, a chyrhaeddodd Dinas y Llyn Halen ac arhosodd hi efo teulu Mr William Williams, Kensington, nes yr oedd yn Ebrill 1880, pan y daeth hi a'i chwaer Polly, yr hon a ymfudodd yn y flwyddyn 1879 i Ddinas y Llyn Halen, pan y daethant i Logan a rhentu lle bychan gan Mrs W. H. Garry, ac yr oedd yn cadw busnes gwnio lle y maent yn bresennol.

Ei rhieni yw Robert ac Ann Davies. Bedyddiwyd hwy yn Rhosllanerchrugog, Cymru, yn y flwyddyn 1855 gan John Berry, Elder. Yn Ebrill y flwyddyn 1858 daethant i fyw i Manchester.

Yn y flwyddyn 1860 daethant i fyw i Liverpool, ac ymfudasant o Liverpool i Utah Hydref 18, 1879, a chyrhaeddasant Utah, Logan, Tachwedd 10, 1879.

Mae dau fab a thair neu bedair o ferched yn y wlad eto heb ddod yma eto.

## Modern English translation

[continuation from PDF page 94]

Miss Davies was born in Rhosllanerchrugog, Denbighshire, Wales, on June 30, 1854.

She was baptized by Henry Hatfield in Liverpool and confirmed by Joseph H. Smith in August 1877.

She emigrated to Utah in 1878 and arrived in Salt Lake City, where she stayed with the family of Mr. William Williams in Kensington until April 1880. Then she and her sister Polly, who had emigrated to Salt Lake City in 1879, came to Logan and rented a small place from Mrs. W. H. Garry, where they kept a sewing business and remain at present.

Her parents are Robert and Ann Davies. They were baptized in Rhosllanerchrugog, Wales, in 1855 by Elder John Berry. In April 1858 they moved to Manchester.

In 1860 they moved to Liverpool, and on October 18, 1879, they emigrated from Liverpool to Utah, arriving in Logan, Utah, on November 10, 1879.

Two sons and three or four daughters remain in the old country and have not yet come here.

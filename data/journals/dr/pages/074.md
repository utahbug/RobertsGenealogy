## Editorial annotations

Page 74 contains three distinct textual blocks and does not continue either signed entry on page 73.

The first two blocks are English family-genealogy records dated within their text to January 14, 1856, February 28, 1858, April 5, 1858, September 11, 1837, and April 4, 1858. Their hand, ink, slant, line spacing, capital forms, numeral forms, repeated `Genealogy` heading, and first-person phrase `by me (David R Roberts)` continue the hand of the signed February 4, 1858 David R Roberts entry on page 73. David R Roberts is therefore the confirmed content author and scribe of these two blocks by strong handwriting continuity plus explicit self-identification. They are contemporaneous family records rather than continuations of the page-73 sentences.

The lower block changes to Welsh, darker ink, heavier pressure, larger spacing, and a less uniform slant. It is a farm/planting register beginning in 1864 and ending cleanly on page 74 with the April 22, 1865 planting line; page 75 begins a separate livestock-loss entry. Because it records post-November 4, 1858 events, it cannot have been newly written by David Roberts. Its hand does not match the signed David R Roberts sample and is not sufficiently close to the dense Robert D. Roberts narrative hand on page 77 for attribution. Its content author and scribe remain an unidentified Roberts-family hand, attribution uncertain.

RDR page 23 is a confirmed same-event parallel for Robert D Roberts's April 4, 1858 baptism at Farmington by his father David R Roberts and confirmation at the water's edge. No clear RDR parallel was located for Jane D Roberts's record or the planting register. RDR was not used to reconstruct uncertain manuscript names, place forms, or Welsh planting terminology.

The signed February 4, 1858 page-73 entry and the self-identified English entries on this page form part of the direct David R Roberts comparison baseline. Together with the signed April 6, 1858 entries on page 76, their recurring letterforms, numerals, spacing, slant, and layout habits confirm the David Roberts scribal attribution of pages 68-72 within the limits of historical manuscript-image comparison.

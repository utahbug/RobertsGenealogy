- Elinor Roberts — [unclear relationship phrase]

- Laura Parry — cyfnither ei mam hi

## Modern English translation

January 29, 1885

Cathreina Thomas Roberts was baptized in the Temple for the following names by Thomas H. Smith and confirmed by Charles C. Olsen:

- Margaret Jones — her female cousin
- Cathreina Williams — wife of her mother's male cousin
- Margaret Owens — her sister-in-law
- Cathreina Jones — wife of her mother's male cousin
- Gwen Williams — wife of her father's male cousin

February 3, 1885

Ann Roberts Hopkins was baptized for the following names by Robert Price and confirmed by N. C. Edlyson:

- Elinor Roberts — [unclear relationship phrase]
- Gweneth Jones — her father's female cousin
- Laura Parry — her mother's female cousin
- Margaret Jones — wife of [unclear: her grandfather]
- Margaret Jones — [unclear relationship phrase]

March 3, 1885

Ann Roberts Hopkins was baptized for the following names by H. C. Smart and confirmed by Thomas H. Smith:

- Elinor Griffith — her father's sister-in-law
- Laura Richards — her mother's female cousin
- Margaret Richards — [ditto]

## Editorial annotations

- Page 107 contains three blank-separated proxy-baptism groups. Each heading identifies the proxy participant, officiators, and date; each subordinate line preserves only its written kinship phrase.
- Explicit terms include `cyfnither` (female cousin), `gwraig` (wife), `chwaer ynghyfraith` (sister-in-law), `mam` (mother), and `tad` (father).
- RDR PDFs 31–32 are confirmed close-paraphrase/normalized parallels. The Welsh headings appear to give January 29, February 3, and March 3, while RDR gives January 27, February 2, and March 2. The dates have not been harmonized.
- RDR reads Jane Roberts, Linet Jones, Margaret Evans, Ann Richards, and other normalized or differing forms where this Welsh page appears to give Elinor Roberts, Gweneth Jones, a repeated Margaret Jones, Laura Richards, and related variants. The Welsh forms remain primary here.
- Two relationship phrases in the February group remain materially uncertain and have not been reconstructed from RDR.
- Handwriting continuity supports probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears.
- PDF page 108 begins a sparse 1886 ordinance list without the same dense kinship annotations.
- No occurrence of `bach` or a distinct Robert-derived nickname is visible.

# journal-dr-page-073

## Welsh transcription

### Entry 1: Elizabeth Roberts genealogy

Logan City April 17 1886

Genealogy of Elizabeth Roberts

Her parents names are David R Roberts and Catherine Thomas she was born [unclear: November 13] in the year 1846 in Cwm y Gelli Parish of Festiniog County of Merioneth North Wales she has been baptised by her father David R Roberts and very likely confirmed by the same April 9 the year 1855.

June 13 the year 1869 she was married by Thomas [unclear: Landon] in her Mothers house to a young man by the name of Taliesin Hughes.

Robert D Roberts

### Entry 2: David R Roberts autobiography

[unclear: Gelli, Davis] Farmington Feb 4th 1858.

Genealogy of David R Roberts. My native land is North Wales my Father was born in Clynog Caernarvonshire and my Mother both in Caernarvon Shire [unclear: she was born in Beddgelert]. I was born in a house called [unclear: Bron y Garth] in a place named [unclear: Nantmor] in Merioneth Shire but as I have said reside with Caernarvon since long time. I was born on the sixth of April 1814 and Married in the year 1837 in the Parish of Llanfrothen Merionethshire North Wales and I was Baptised May 24th in 1846 by Robert Evans Glan Conwy and in June 21st being Confirmed by Abel Evans March in Priest and soon after that to be an Elder and emigrated from the old country on the 13th of April in the 1856 and arrived the Valley on the 2nd Oct 1856.

David R Roberts

## Editorial annotations

Page 73 does not continue page 72. It contains two distinct English entries in different hands and from different writing moments.

The upper entry is dated April 17, 1886, concerns Elizabeth Roberts, and is explicitly signed `Robert D Roberts`. Robert D. Roberts is therefore the confirmed content author and scribe of that entry. The account is retrospective and records events from 1846, 1855, and 1869.

The lower entry is dated February 4, 1858, is written in the first person as a genealogy/autobiography of David R Roberts, and is explicitly signed `David R Roberts`. David R. Roberts is therefore the confirmed content author and scribe of that entry. Its date precedes David Roberts's death on November 4, 1858. The hand differs from the upper Robert D. Roberts entry and is broadly consistent with the compact early Welsh block on pages 68-72, but the signature and first-person self-identification provide the direct attribution evidence.

The lower entry supplies an independent manuscript witness for April 6, 1814 as David Roberts's birth date. This agrees with the day visible in Welsh on page 68 and conflicts with the April 14 date in the later RDR historical English account; the disagreement is preserved rather than harmonized.

The RDR journal contains related family-history material, but no passage located in the completed transcription is a close page-level duplicate of these two entries. RDR page 23 independently records Robert D. Roberts's April 4, 1858 baptism by his father David R. Roberts; that is contextual corroboration, not a parallel for the events transcribed here.

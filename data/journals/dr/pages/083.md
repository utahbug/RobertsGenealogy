## Welsh transcription

Logan City, November 5, 1869

Brynhawn dydd Llun, Hydref yr unfed ar ddeg (11), y flwyddyn 1869, rhoddodd Elizabeth ein chwaer, gwraig Taliesin Hughes, ei chenedigaeth a gefeilliaid, bachgen a merch, ond ni ddaethant i fyw ond ychydig o amser. Elizabeth a fu yn afiachus wrth dacluso gwely ei brawd ynghyfraith, yr hwn a oedd ar le uchel yn gofyn; yr hyn a achosodd eu hanamserol enedigaeth. Mae Elizabeth wedi gwella yn rhagorol o dda. Mi yr ydym am eu henwau, sef James a Margaret Hughes.

Maent yn bwriadu myned i Ddinas y Llyn Halen yn niwedd y flwyddyn i gael eu golchi a'u heneinio yn nhy yr Arglwydd ar yr [unclear: unfed ar hugain] (22) o Dachwedd 1869. Maent yn byw yn Meadow Valley, Sevier County, Utah Territory.

R. D. Roberts

Ar yr ail ar hugain (22) o Dachwedd 1869, yn lle yr bymtheg (15), y cawsant eu [unclear: hymgysegru] yn nhy yr Arglwydd yn Ninas y Llyn Halen.

## Modern English translation

Logan City, November 5, 1869

On Monday afternoon, October 11, in the year 1869, Elizabeth, our sister and the wife of Taliesin Hughes, gave birth to twins, a boy and a girl, but they lived only a short time. Elizabeth had been unwell while arranging her brother-in-law's bed, which was required to be in a high place; this caused their premature birth. Elizabeth has recovered exceptionally well. We give their names as James and Margaret Hughes.

They intend to go to Salt Lake City at the end of the year to be washed and anointed in the house of the Lord on [unclear in Welsh: the twenty-first] (22) of November 1869. They live in Meadow Valley, Sevier County, Utah Territory.

R. D. Roberts

Logan City, January 1870

There is an error in the account above concerning the twins; their names are James and Cathrina, Cathrina having the same name as the mother of Elizabeth, our sister.

On November 22, 1869, instead of the fifteenth (15), they were [unclear in Welsh: consecrated] in the house of the Lord in Salt Lake City.

Robert D. Roberts

[Unclear in Welsh: A house was taken] from William Cowley, and on June 11, 1870, Hannah Roberts and I went to live together for the first time. Everything is well now. July 14, 1870.

## Editorial annotations

- Page 83 does not continue the copied family Bible register on page 82. It begins a new November 1869 first-person family entry.
- The November 5, 1869 entry has a confirmed parallel on RDR PDF page 11. RDR is a close paraphrase but incorporates the January 1870 correction below: it gives the girl's name as `Cathreina` rather than the initially written `Margaret`, and it gives November 22 for the planned temple visit.
- The Welsh first writes an ordinal that appears inconsistent with the parenthetical `22`; both are preserved rather than silently harmonized. The January 1870 correction explicitly contrasts November 22 with the fifteenth.
- The January 1870 correction has a confirmed conceptual parallel in RDR PDF page 11, although RDR folds the corrected facts into its main narrative rather than preserving the correction as a separate entry.
- The closing June/July 1870 household note has a confirmed parallel on RDR PDF page 12. RDR expands the opening into `I rent a house of William M Cowly` and supplies Salt Lake City; the Welsh opening remains uncertain and the RDR expansion has not been substituted for it.

# journal-dr-page-097

## Welsh transcription

Logan City Chwefror 1af 1883

Mi anfonodd lythyr at [unclear: deulu ni] Conar Hughes yn ein hysbysu fod ei wraig Jane, ein chwaer, wedi rhoi genedigaeth i fachgen ar Ionawr 17eg 1883 am ugain munud wedi un (20.1) yn y bore. Bendithiwyd ef gan David R. Bowen Mawrth 1af, ei enw Robert Richard Hughes.

Logan City Ionawr 4ydd 1884

Er cofawdriaeth am Robert Davies a aned yn Rhuthun, Sir Ddinbych, Cymru, Chwefror 25ain 1819. Bedyddiwyd ef yn Rhuthun gan John Parry yn y flwyddyn 1853. Bu farw yn Logan City Ionawr 2ail 1884 am ddeng munud wedi un yn y bore (1.10). Claddwyd ef ar y 3ydd. Agorwyd y cwrdd gan yr Esgob Henry Ballard, cyhoeddwyd gan Georg Painter, bregethwyd gan yr Esgob Ballard. Ei gynghorwyr Christian L. Larson mewn modd dymunol a chymerth amrywiaeth o ganiadau gan Georg Morrison, diweddiwyd gan John Thomas. Gwasanaenwyd y te gan Christian L. Larson.

Robert Davies yw dad ynghyfraith Thomas J. Roberts.

## Modern English translation

Logan City, February 1, 1883

Conar Hughes sent a letter to [unclear in Welsh], informing us that his wife Jane, our sister, had given birth to a boy on January 17, 1883, at twenty minutes past one in the morning. He was blessed by David R. Bowen on March 1 and named Robert Richard Hughes.

Logan City, January 4, 1884

In memory of Robert Davies, who was born in Ruthin, Denbighshire, Wales, on February 25, 1819. He was baptized in Ruthin by John Parry in 1853. He died in Logan City on January 2, 1884, at ten minutes past one in the morning. He was buried on the third. The meeting was opened by Bishop Henry Ballard; [unclear in Welsh] by Georg Painter; Bishop Ballard preached. His counselor Christian L. Larson [spoke] in a pleasing manner, and a variety of songs were provided by Georg Morrison. John Thomas closed the meeting. Tea was served by Christian L. Larson.

Robert Davies is the father-in-law of Thomas J. Roberts.

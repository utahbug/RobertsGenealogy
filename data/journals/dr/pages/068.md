Fy Hanes

Wele a ganlyn sydd ychydig o'm hachau fel y gallais eu cael.

Ganwyd fi yn y flwyddyn 1814 Ebrill y 5 yn Sir Feirionydd yn y [unclear: Nanmor] mewn ty a elwir y Rhos hwn ar hen etifeddiaeth yr enwog Arglwydd [unclear: Dafydd Nanmor] (yr hwn y lle yn awr ag Beddgelert yn Sir Gaernarfon). ## Modern English translation

January 6, 1854        David Roberts

My history

Behold, what follows is a little of my ancestry, as I was able to obtain it.

I was born on April 5, 1814, in Merionethshire, in [unclear place in Welsh], in a house called Rhos, on the old estate of the renowned Lord [unclear name in Welsh] (the place is now associated with Beddgelert in Caernarfonshire). Eight children were born to our parents: Robert, who died at three months old; Edward; Robert; Elinor; David; William; Daniel; and John Roberts. The five eldest were born at Tanyrallt, the next two at Brynygwyngyll, and the youngest son, John, at Llety in the parish of Llanfrothen.

Edward married Elinor Griffith, daughter of Griffith and Chatherine Williams of Bronyforllan in Llanfrothen.

Robert married Gwen in Llanfrothen. She was the daughter of William and Mary Williams of Cae Glas, Llanfrothen. Twelve children were born to them.

Elinor married John, son of William Humphrey and Chatherine James of the parish of Festiniog. Nine children were born to her; they emigrated to America.

I, David, married Chathreina, daughter of Thomas Richards and Ann Morris of Creua Mawr, Llanfrothen. Six children were born to us: the eldest, Robert, followed by Thomas, David, Ann, Elizabeth, and Jane Roberts.

William married Margred, daughter of Evan Roberts and Jane Griffith of Gair y Rallt bach. Four children were born to them: Elizabeth, Robert, Evan, and William Roberts; the youngest died as an infant.

Daniel married Gwen in Llanfrothen, daughter of John Lloyd and Chatherine [continues on PDF page 69].

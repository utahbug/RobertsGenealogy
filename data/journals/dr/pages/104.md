## Modern English translation

Logan City, February [unclear: 21], 1885

The following names were married and sealed in the everlasting covenant and to the blessings of the Gospel by A. C. Tellefsen:

Laura Parry and Cathreina Richards to David Robert Roberts

Proxies: Robert D. Roberts and Cathreina Thomas Roberts

Linet Jones and Jane Richards to John Roberts

Ann Roberts to Robert Parry

Ann Parry to Edward Griffith

Proxies: [unclear: Robert D. Roberts] and Ann Roberts Hopkins

Margaret Owens to Richard Thomas

Proxies: Robert D. Roberts and Cathreina Thomas Roberts

March 12, 1886 — Jane Thomas to Morris Isaac

Margaret Jones to William Hughes

Proxies: Robert David Roberts and Cathreina Thomas Roberts

## Editorial annotations

- The heading uses `briodwyd ac a seliwyd` (“were married and sealed”) and `Cyfamod tragwyddol` (“everlasting covenant”). The manuscript then groups named sealing subjects with explicit `Dirprwywyr` (“proxies”) lines.
- The page is not a simple two-column husband/wife register. Several groups contain more than two subject names before the proxy line; their exact relationship has not been inferred.
- RDR PDF page 29 is a confirmed close-paraphrase/normalized parallel. RDR reorganizes the opening names and supplies clearer modern ordinance prose, but that arrangement has not replaced the Welsh manuscript order.
- The heading date remains uncertain in the Welsh image; RDR gives February 21, 1885.
- Source forms including `Laura Parry`, `Cathreina Richards`, `Linet Jones`, and `Cathreina Thomas Roberts` remain unnormalized.
- Handwriting continuity supports probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears.
- No occurrence of `bach` or a distinct Robert-derived nickname is visible.

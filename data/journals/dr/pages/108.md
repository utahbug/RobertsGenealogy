## Editorial annotations

- Page 108 contains two sparse March 1886 ordinance groups. It does not continue page 107's dense kinship lists, and no explicit family relationships are written here.
- The first group names Margaret Goodman Davies Roberts as proxy/participant and then lists represented women using ditto structure. The opening represented-name sequence remains uncertain.
- The second group names Thomas D. Roberts and three represented men. Confirmation is explicit on the William Jones line; the repeated ordinance context and the Griffith Jones wording remain partly uncertain.
- RDR PDF page 28 is a confirmed close-paraphrase/normalized parallel for the first group. RDR PDF page 26 is a probable reordered parallel for the second group, but differs in date grouping, Henry Jones/Lewis, and the Merrill initials. The Welsh has not been conformed to RDR.
- Source forms `Linet Morris`, `Cathreine Jones`, and `M. D. Merrill` remain as written.
- Handwriting continuity supports probable attribution to Robert D. Roberts as content author and scribe. No signature or secondary hand appears.
- No occurrence of `bach` or a distinct Robert-derived nickname is visible.

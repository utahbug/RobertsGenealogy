ac un y dydd o'r blaen [unclear: tair mlwyddian dlwy ar nod]

## Modern English translation

### Entry 1: livestock losses, 1865

1865, on the seventh of April, two cows died,

and one the day before [unclear: three years ...].

### Entry 2: death of the old cow, 1875

1875, the old cow died of old age at nineteen years old, January 24.

## Editorial annotations

Page 75 contains two separate Welsh livestock-loss entries. Contrary to the preliminary continuity hypothesis, page 74's planting register ends cleanly with its April 22, 1865 planting line; page 75 begins a new livestock entry dated April 7, 1865. The first entry records two cows dying on April 7 and another on the preceding day. Its final age/count phrase remains unclear and has not been expanded from context.

The second entry is separately dated January 24, 1875 and records an old cow dying of old age at nineteen years. It is a brief farm-diary notice, not an accounting transaction, purchase, sale, or family-history entry.

Both entries share the darker ink, heavy pressure, larger line height, broad spacing, numeral construction, and less uniform slant of page 74's lower Welsh farm register. They are probably by the same unidentified Roberts-family content author and scribe. They do not match the confirmed signed David R Roberts hand on page 73 and the English part of page 74, and their 1865/1875 writing dates postdate David Roberts's death. They are also not sufficiently close to the dense Robert D Roberts narrative hand on pages 77 onward for attribution.

Neither entry continues onto page 76. Page 76 begins a new English source block dated April 6, 1858, signed David R Roberts, and later changes to a Welsh narrative block.

No clear RDR parallel was found for either livestock entry. RDR was not used to reconstruct the unresolved first-entry phrase.

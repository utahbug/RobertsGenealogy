Mawrth 30, 1871, am ddeugain munud wedi wyth (8.40) yn yr hwyr, Hannah Roberts fy ngwraig a roddodd enedigaeth i fab cryf a bywiog. Yr oedd ei lais mor lan a'r awel y boreu; naw pwys (9 lbs). Ei enw yw David (neu Deffy) Robert Roberts. Bendithiwyd ef gan ei daid Hugh Roberts, Ebrill 15, 1871.

R. D. Roberts

Mawrth 2, 1872

Chwefror 26 daeth Alizander Lewis o Samaria, Oneida County, Idaho Territory, a newydd drwg o farwolaeth Margaret, un o efeilliaid Elizabeth ein chwaer a Taliesin Hughes. ## Modern English translation

[continuation from PDF page 84]

Margaret was weak and appeared unlikely to live long, when she [unclear in Welsh], but she is now growing stronger. Both were blessed on February 9, 1871, by Thomas J. Thomas.

R. D. Roberts

April 10, 1871

On March 30, 1871, at forty minutes past eight (8:40) in the evening, Hannah Roberts, my wife, gave birth to a strong and lively son. His voice was as clear as the morning breeze; he weighed nine pounds. His name is David (or Deffy) Robert Roberts. He was blessed by his grandfather Hugh Roberts on April 15, 1871.

R. D. Roberts

March 2, 1872

On February 26, Alizander Lewis came from Samaria, Oneida County, Idaho Territory, with the bad news of the death of Margaret, one of the twins of Elizabeth, our sister, and Taliesin Hughes. On the 28th I received a letter containing the same sorrow. The little one had caught a severe cold; she appeared to be improving, but suffered fits and departed on February 23, 1872. Her companion is ill with the cold, but we do not know how she is at present.

R. D. Roberts

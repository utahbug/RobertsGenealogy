# Annotated Book of Remembrance: Edition Scope

## Shared Family Edition

The shared historical edition will contain the common Roberts-family core:

- Welsh ancestry
- David and Chathreina/Catherine Roberts
- conversion and migration
- Robert D. Roberts
- major children and family context
- a concise biography of David D. Roberts, compiler of the Book of Remembrance

This edition will preserve original compiler narrative, manuscript/source layers, editorial corrections, conflicts, and evidence notes without duplicating later immediate-family detail.

## Immediate Family Extension

A later companion edition, intended primarily for siblings and close descendants, will extend the shared core through:

- Thomas Roberts
- later descendants
- Wendell Roberts
- immediate-family photographs and documents

The extension should reference rather than duplicate the shared historical core.

## Reserved compiler biography

### David D. Roberts - Compiler of the Book of Remembrance

Future research topics:

- his role as compiler and custodian of inherited family records
- his family-history and genealogical work
- public service
- Utah state legislative service
- the name `Good Roads Roberts`
- the relevant state-archives article
- why record keeping and family history mattered to him

No dates or biographical claims should be added until supported by identified sources.
